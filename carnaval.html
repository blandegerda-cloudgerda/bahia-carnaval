Carnaval da Bahia

Salvador

Carnaval da Bahia is one of the biggest street festivals in Brazil. It takes place in Salvador, the capital of Bahia.

Music

The carnival is famous for axé music, samba and large sound trucks called trio elétricos.

When?

The main carnival takes place around February or March, depending on the date of Easter.

Some carnival words

  • Axé
  • Trio elétrico
  • Bloco
  • Olodum
