Carnaval da Bahia

Carnaval da Bahia is a famous street festival in Salvador, Brazil.

Salvador

Salvador is the capital of Bahia and one of the main places to experience Carnaval in Brazil.

Music

The festival is known for axé music, samba and trio elétricos – large trucks with musicians and speakers.

Important words

  • Axé
  • Trio elétrico
  • Bloco
  • Olodum

When does it happen?

Carnaval usually takes place in February or March, depending on the date of Easter.
